01 // INTRODUCTION

Every project is a story.

Scriitor is the library that keeps yours.

A plain-text, git-backed memory system for long-lived work threads, designed for harmonious collaboration between humans and AI assistants.

02 // THE PROBLEM

Where were we?

You leave a thread for six weeks, and the reasoning scatters: your head, somebody else's head, a chat transcript nobody will read again. That is a tax, and you pay it on every return.

Your AI assistants pay it on every conversation, because they begin each one knowing nothing.

LEXICON & TRANSLATION

Scriitor [skree-tor]

noun (Romanian) — writer

Scriitor is a library for work that never closes, and scrii is its librarian.

A story is a long-lived thread: a customer, a product line, an integration. A chapter opens whenever a story stirs, and fills with plain Markdown pages. All of it lives in an ordinary git repository.

03 // ARCHITECTURAL PILASTERS

Designed for longevity. Engineered for truth.

01 / STORIES

Stories never close

A page can be finished. A chapter ends where the writing ends. A story never closes. Nothing asks you to predict that an issue will not come back, so when it does, months later, it is simply the next chapter.

02 / RETRIEVAL

Ask, and get exactly what you need

Not the whole shelf: the memory that belongs to you, to the story, and to the chapter, gathered into one answer. A human and an AI assistant are both patrons here, with different needs, and the library answers both.

03 / SPEED

Nothing blocks you, and nothing is forgotten

No gates, no ceremony, no you cannot proceed until. A problem becomes a plain fact on disk, in sight until it is genuinely fixed.

04 / COOPERATION

Notes, not locks

One repository, readable by everyone on it. When you work across stories you borrow: a note saying what you have open, so nobody writes over what you are reading. No locks, no permissions to administer, ever.

05 / INDEPENDENCE

Plain files, yours to keep

Every file is plain text you can open, understand, and correct yourself. Nothing you write depends on Scriitor still being here in ten years.

SPECIFICATION

"The record of reasoning is as valuable as the binary itself."
